Right to Repair

In October 2024 the US Copyright Office granted a DMCA exemption for repairing retail food-prep equipment, soft serve machines included. Before that, only manufacturer-authorised technicians could get into the machine. Every one of our vans carries a Taylor tune-up kit. Flip the switch to see who could actually use it.

"It's cleaning"

The machines run a nightly heat-treatment cycle, heating the mix to about 151 °F and refreezing it, that can take up to four hours. If it fails, the machine is down until a technician arrives.
